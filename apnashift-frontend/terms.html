Disclaimer: ApnaShift abhi testing/beta phase me hai. Niyam simple rakhe gaye hain aur waqt ke saath badal sakte hain.

1. Service kya hai

ApnaShift ek platform hai jo customers ko independent drivers se connect karta hai shifting aur transport ke liye (pickup, mini truck, mini tractor jaise options). Hum khud gaadi nahi chalate — booking, price andaza aur driver matching ki suvidha dete hain.

2. Booking aur cancellation

  • Booking confirm hone se pehle tum kabhi bhi free me cancel kar sakte ho.
  • Booking confirm hone ke baad agar cancel karte ho to abhi testing phase me koi charge nahi lagta.
  • Driver ke pahunchne ke baad cancel karna ho to driver se seedha baat karo — uska time kharab na ho, iska dhyaan rakho.

3. Payment

  • Payment directly driver ko karo — cash ya UPI se.
  • Abhi ApnaShift me koi in-app payment nahi hai, isliye app tumse paise nahi legi.
  • Booking par dikha price sirf andaza hai; final kiraya driver ke saath trip se pehle pakka kar lo.

4. Responsibility — driver vs ApnaShift

  • Driver ki responsibility: samaan savdhani se load, transport aur unload karna; time par pahunchna.
  • ApnaShift ki responsibility: booking milana, price ka andaza dena aur support me madad karna.
  • Saaf shabdon me: samaan ka damage ya loss hone par ApnaShift abhi koi insurance provide nahi karta. Aisa kuch ho to pehle driver se baat karo, phir humein support par likho — hum sulah karane me madad karenge, par nuksaan ki bharpai ki guarantee nahi dete.

5. Account suspend / ban

Ghalat phone number, fake booking, driver se badtameezi ya system ka misuse karne par ApnaShift ke paas tumhara account suspend ya ban karne ka adhikaar hai — bina pehle bataye bhi, agar misuse gambhir ho.

6. Dispute resolution

Koi bhi jhagda ya shikayat ho to pehle support@apnashift.in par contact karo. Hum dono pakshon ki baat sunkar hal nikalne ki koshish karenge. Baatcheet se hal na nikle to Indore (Madhya Pradesh) ka kanoon lagu hoga.
