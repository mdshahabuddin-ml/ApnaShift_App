Help & Support

Frequently asked questions — tap an answer to expand it.

How do I create a booking?

Create an ApnaShift account, enter your pickup and drop locations, choose a vehicle, and confirm your booking. You will receive an update as soon as a driver is assigned.

What should I do if no driver is available?

Please wait a short while and try again — it may take longer to find a driver during periods of high demand. You can also try a different vehicle type.

How do I cancel a booking?

You can cancel free of charge at any time before your booking is confirmed. During the testing phase, cancelling a confirmed booking is also free of charge.

How do payments work?

Please pay the driver directly — by cash or UPI. ApnaShift does not currently offer in-app payments.

How do I raise a complaint?

Complete the form below — your complaint will reach us via WhatsApp. Please include your booking ID so we can help you quickly.
