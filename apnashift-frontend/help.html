Help & Support

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal — jawab ke liye tap karo.

Booking kaise karein?

ApnaShift par account banao, pickup aur drop location dalo, gaadi chuno aur booking confirm karo. Driver assign hote hi tumhein update mil jayega.

Driver available nahi hai to kya karein?

Thodi der rukkar dobara try karo — demand zyada hone par driver milne me time lag sakta hai. Gaadi type badalkar bhi dekh sakte ho.

Cancel kaise karein?

Booking confirm hone se pehle kabhi bhi free me cancel kar sakte ho. Testing phase me confirm booking cancel par bhi koi charge nahi lagta.

Payment kaise hota hai?

Payment directly driver ko karo — cash ya UPI se. Abhi ApnaShift me in-app payment nahi hai.

Complaint kaise karein?

Neeche wala form bharo — WhatsApp par tumhari shikayat hum tak pahunch jayegi. Booking ID likhna mat bhoolo taaki hum jaldi madad kar saken.

Humse contact karo

Form bharo — WhatsApp khulega, bas Send dabana hai.

10 digit ka Indian mobile. +91 ya space chalega.
