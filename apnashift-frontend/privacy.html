Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ApnaShift ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and what your rights are — in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

1. What data we collect

  • Name — to identify your account and bookings.
  • Phone number — for login, booking updates, and support communication.
  • Pickup and drop addresses — to complete your shifting booking.
  • Booking history — a record of your past and ongoing bookings.
  • Vehicle details (for drivers) — registration details such as vehicle type, vehicle number, license, and service area.

2. Why we collect it

  • To process bookings — your details are required for pickup, pricing, and scheduling.
  • To assign a driver — address and requirement data is used to match you with a driver with the right vehicle and service area.
  • For support — to contact you and provide assistance if any issue arises.

We do not use your data for any purpose other than those described here.

3. Who we share it with

  • Only the information required for your booking — for example, the assigned driver receives the pickup/drop address and phone number, and the customer receives the driver's name and vehicle number.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties — your personal data is never shared for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. How long we store your data

Your data is retained only for as long as necessary for bookings, support, and legal record-keeping. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems (except for records we are legally required to retain).

5. Your rights

You have the right to view, update, and request deletion of your data. To do so, please contact us:

  • Email: support@apnashift.in
  • Send a message containing "data request" from your registered phone number so we can verify your identity.

6. Security

  • Passwords are stored in hashed form — we can never view or read your actual password.
  • Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) so it cannot be read in transit.
  • Internal access is limited to authorized staff only.

7. Questions

If you have any questions about privacy, please write to support@apnashift.in — we will respond.
