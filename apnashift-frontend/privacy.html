Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ApnaShift ("hum", "hamara") tumhari privacy ka sammaan karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kya data lete hain, kyun lete hain, aur tumhare adhikaar kya hain — India ke Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) ke hisaab se.

1. Hum kya data collect karte hain

  • Naam — account aur booking pehchaan ke liye.
  • Phone number — login, booking updates aur support contact ke liye.
  • Pickup/drop address — shifting booking complete karne ke liye.
  • Booking history — tumhari purani aur chal rahi bookings ka record.
  • Vehicle details (drivers ke liye) — gaadi type, gaadi number, license aur service area jaise registration details.

2. Kyun collect karte hain

  • Booking process karne ke liye — pickup, pricing aur scheduling ke liye tumhari details zaroori hain.
  • Driver assign karne ke liye — sahi gaadi aur area ka driver milane ke liye address aur requirement data use hota hai.
  • Support ke liye — koi dikkat aaye to tumse contact karne aur madad dene ke liye.

Hum tumhara data in kaamon ke alawa kisi aur maksad ke liye use nahi karte.

3. Kisse share karte hain

  • Sirf booking ke liye zaroori info — jaise assigned driver ko pickup/drop address aur phone, ya customer ko driver ka naam aur gaadi number.
  • Kisi third-party ko bechte nahi — tumhara personal data advertising ya marketing ke liye kisi ko nahi diya jata.

4. Kitna time store karte hain

Tumhara data utne hi time tak rakha jata hai jitna booking, support aur kanooni record ke liye zaroori ho. Account delete karne par tumhara personal data hamare active system se hata diya jata hai (kanoon ke tahat rakhna zaroori record chhodkar).

5. Tumhare adhikaar

Tumhein apna data dekhne, update karne aur delete request karne ka adhikaar hai. Iske liye humein contact karo:

  • Email: support@apnashift.in
  • Apne registered phone number se "data request" likhkar bhejo taaki hum tumhari pehchaan confirm kar saken.

6. Security

  • Password hash hoke store hota hai — hum tumhara asli password kabhi dekh ya padh nahi sakte.
  • Data encrypted connection (HTTPS) par bheja jata hai taaki raste me koi padh na sake.
  • Internal access sirf zaroori staff tak limited hai.

7. Sawal ho to

Privacy se juda koi bhi sawal ho to support@apnashift.in par likho — hum jawab denge.
