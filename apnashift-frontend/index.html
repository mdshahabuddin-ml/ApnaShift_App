Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap for answers.

How do I book?

Log in, enter pickup and drop, choose a vehicle, and confirm. You will get an update once a driver is assigned.

How will I get a driver?

After booking, a nearby verified driver is assigned. You will see the driver's name and phone.

How do I cancel?

Cancel for free before confirmation. To cancel after confirmation, talk to the driver and write to us on the Help page.

How do I pay?

Pay the driver directly by cash or UPI. The app has no online payment yet.

Where do I complain?

Fill the form on the Help page and mention your booking ID so we can help quickly.
