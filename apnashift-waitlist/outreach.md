- Sirf itna kaho: **waitlist join karo, launch par special rate milega**.
- ApnaShift abhi testing me hai — jo pooche use sach batao.
- Har template me `{LINK}` ki jagah links.md wala tracking link paste karo.

## 1. WhatsApp status (bahut chhota)

```
Ghar badlo, tension nahi! ApnaShift waitlist join karo, launch par special rate milega: {LINK}
```

Best time/jagah: Subah 9–11 ya shaam 7–9 baje status lagao, 24 ghante rakho.

