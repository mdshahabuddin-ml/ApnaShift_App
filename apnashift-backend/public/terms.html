Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Disclaimer: ApnaShift is currently in a testing/beta phase. These terms are kept simple and may change over time.

1. What the service is

ApnaShift is a platform that connects customers with independent drivers for shifting and transportation (including options such as pickup, mini truck, and mini tractor). We do not operate vehicles ourselves — we provide booking, price estimates, and driver-matching services.

2. Booking and cancellation

  • You can cancel free of charge at any time before your booking is confirmed.
  • If you cancel after your booking is confirmed, no charge currently applies during the testing phase.
  • If you need to cancel after the driver has arrived, please speak directly with the driver — and be mindful of their time.

3. Payment

  • Please pay the driver directly — by cash or UPI.
  • ApnaShift currently offers no in-app payments, so the app will not charge you.
  • The price shown for a booking is only an estimate; please confirm the final fare with the driver before the trip.

4. Responsibilities — driver vs ApnaShift

  • Driver responsibilities: loading, transporting, and unloading goods carefully, and arriving on time.
  • ApnaShift responsibilities: arranging bookings, providing price estimates, and assisting with support.
  • To be clear: ApnaShift currently provides no insurance for damage to or loss of goods. If this occurs, please first speak with the driver, then contact our support team — we will help facilitate a resolution, but we do not guarantee compensation for losses.

5. Account suspension and bans

Providing an incorrect phone number, creating fake bookings, behaving inappropriately with a driver, or misusing the system may result in ApnaShift suspending or banning your account — including without prior notice in cases of serious misuse.

6. Dispute resolution

If you have any dispute or complaint, please first contact support@apnashift.in. We will hear both sides and attempt to reach a resolution. If the matter cannot be resolved through discussion, the laws applicable in Indore (Madhya Pradesh) will apply.
